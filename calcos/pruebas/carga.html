Tiempos y errores de calcos-datos por minuto (Logs → SQL) 
select toStartOfMinute(timestamp) as minuto, count(*) as llamadas,
       countIf(toInt32OrZero(log_attributes['response.status_code']) >= 500) as errores,
       round(avg(toFloat64OrZero(log_attributes['execution_time_ms']))) as prom_ms,
       max(toFloat64OrZero(log_attributes['execution_time_ms'])) as max_ms
from logs
where source = 'function_edge_logs'
  and log_attributes['request.pathname'] like '%calcos-datos%'
group by minuto order by minuto desc

Si esa consulta no corre en el editor de Logs, pasale a Claude el resumen de esta página (📋) con la hora: él la corre contra los registros.
